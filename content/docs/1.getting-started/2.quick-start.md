# Quick Start

## Step 1: Get an invitation

Innovators Parts has no open signup. An admin or owner of your team invites you
by email address, and that invitation is what lets you in.

Ask an admin to invite you from **Organization → Invite a Member**.

::note
The invitation is tied to the exact address it was sent to. Sign in with a
different address — including a different Google account — and you will be turned
away, because as far as the app can tell you were never invited.
::

## Step 2: Create your account

Two ways in, both fine:

- **Continue with Google** on the sign-in page, using the invited address
- **The link in the invitation email**, which opens a signup form with your
  address already filled in — choose a password and you are done

Either way you land in your team's organization straight away. There is nothing
to set up and no invitation to go and accept afterwards.

::tip
Already have an account? Just sign in. The invitation is picked up
automatically.
::

## Step 3: Add your first part

1. Click **Add part**
2. Paste the product URL into **External link**

For many vendors that is enough — the name, SKU and price are filled in for you.
Fill in anything that is missing, set the quantity, and save.

Your part joins that vendor's open order, or starts one if there isn't an open
order for that vendor yet. Add parts from two vendors and you get two orders, one
per checkout you will actually do.

::tip
The most useful habit is pasting the link first and letting the lookup do the
typing.
::

## Step 4: Buy it

When the order is ready, open it and click **Order from vendor**. Where the
vendor supports it, your cart is built in one click; otherwise you get a list of
links to work through in the same tab.

Once you have paid, open **Shipping & payment details** and record the tracking
number, the shipping and tax, and how it was paid — a card, a Kit of Parts
voucher, or a mix. Attach the receipt while you have it in front of you; finding
it again in March is nobody's idea of fun.

## Step 5: Move it along

Drag the order between board columns as it progresses — **To order**, then
**Ordered**, then **Arrived**. The whole order moves together, because that is
how the box actually turns up.

## Step 6: Invite the rest of the team

Admins and owners can bring everyone else in from **Organization → Invite a
Member**. Choose **Admin** for people who should manage tags and members, or
**Member** for everyone else.

Since signups are invitation-only, this is the only way to add someone — nobody
can create an account on their own.
