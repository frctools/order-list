# Quick Start

This guide will help you get started with Innovators Parts in just a few minutes.

## Step 1: Get an Invitation

Innovators Parts does not have open signups. An admin or owner of your team
invites you by email address, and that invitation is what lets you create an
account.

Ask an admin to invite you from **Organization -> Members -> Invite member**.

::note
The invitation is tied to the exact address it was sent to, so you have to sign
up with that address.
::

## Step 2: Create Your Account

Open the link in the invitation email. It takes you to a signup form with your
email already filled in:

1. Enter your **name**
2. Create a **password**
3. Click **Create account**

You are dropped straight into your team's organization once the account is
created -- there is nothing to set up.

::tip
If you already have an account, just log in first and the invitation link adds
you to the organization.
::

## Step 3: Invite Team Members

Admins and owners can bring the rest of the team in:

1. Go to the **Organization** page
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter the **email address** of the team member
4. Select their **role** (Admin or Member)
5. Click **Send Invitation**

The invited member receives an email with a link to join. Because signups are
invitation-only, this is the only way to add someone -- they cannot create an
account on their own.

## Step 4: Create Your First Order

Now you're ready to start tracking orders:

1. Click the **Add Order** button
2. Fill in the order details:
   - **Part Name** - The name of the part you need
   - **Description** - Optional notes about the part
   - **Quantity** - How many you need
   - **Tags** - Optional categorization
3. Click **Create**

::tip
Many popular FRC vendors have automatic information fetching! When you enter a vendor URL, Innovators Parts will try to fill in details like part name and price for you.
::

Your order will be created with a "To Order" status.

## Step 5: Track Order Progress

As your order progresses, update its status:

- **To Order** - Part needs to be ordered
- **Ordered** - Part has been ordered, waiting for arrival
- **Arrived** - Part has arrived and is available

Simply drag orders between columns to change their status.
