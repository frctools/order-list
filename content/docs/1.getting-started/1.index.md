# Welcome to Innovators Parts

Innovators Parts tracks what your FRC team needs to buy, what has been bought,
and what has turned up — with the paperwork attached.

## How it works

Parts are grouped into **orders**, one per vendor. You add a part and say where
it comes from; it joins that vendor's open order, or starts one. That mirrors
what actually happens at the till: one checkout per vendor, not one per part.

An order then moves as a unit — **To order**, **Ordered**, **Arrived** — because
the box arrives, not individual parts.

## Getting started

1. **Ask a team admin for an invitation** — there is no open signup
2. **Sign in with Google**, or use the link in the invitation email
3. **Add a part** by pasting its product URL
4. **Order it**, then record the payment and attach the receipt

::tip
The [Quick Start](/docs/getting-started/quick-start) walks through all of that
in a few minutes.
::
