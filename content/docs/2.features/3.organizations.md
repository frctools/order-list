# Organizations

An organization is your team. Everything — orders, parts, tags, receipts —
belongs to one, and is visible only to its members.

## Getting in

There is no open signup and no self-serve organization creation. An admin or
owner invites an email address, and that invitation is the only way an account
can be created.

That is a deliberate trade: your team's purchasing, prices and receipts are not
something a stranger who finds the URL should be able to browse.

## Roles

| Role       | Can do                                                                 |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Everything, including managing members and roles                       |
| **Admin**  | Everything a member can, plus manage tags, invite people and set roles |
| **Member** | Add, edit, move and delete parts, orders and receipts                  |

The practical differences are narrow. Members do everything to do with buying
things; admins additionally manage **tags**, **members** and the **Organization**
page, which members cannot open at all.

::warning
Members can delete orders and receipts. There is no approval step and no
undo — anyone you invite can remove a purchase record. Bear that in mind when
choosing who to invite while you are keeping receipts for an audit.
::

## Inviting someone

1. Open the **Organization** page
2. Go to **Invite a Member**
3. Enter their **email address**
4. Choose a **role**
5. Send the invitation

They get an email with a link. They can use it — or simply sign in with Google
using that same address, which works just as well.

::note
The invitation is tied to the exact address. If you invite someone's school
address and they sign in with a personal Google account, they are refused, and
the message will tell them they have not been invited. Invite the address they
will actually sign in with.
::

## Accepting an invitation

There is nothing to accept. Sign in — however you like — and the invitation is
applied, along with whatever role it carried. The first thing you see is your
team's orders.

This also repairs itself: if someone somehow ends up with an account but no
organization, signing in again picks up the pending invitation.

## Managing members

From the **Organization** page, owners and admins can:

- **Change a role** — pick a new one from the member's role dropdown
- **Remove a member** — access ends immediately

::warning
Removing a member revokes access at once, but leaves everything they created.
Their orders, parts and receipts stay with the organization, which is what you
want when a student graduates mid-season.
::

## Switching organizations

If you belong to more than one, use the organization selector in the header.
Orders, tags and receipts all follow the switch.

With a single organization there is nothing to choose — you are put into it
automatically when you sign in.
