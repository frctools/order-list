# Import from Onshape

Innovators Parts supports importing your Bill of Materials (BOM) directly from Onshape, making it easy to create orders for all the parts in your robot design.

## Exporting from Onshape

To export your BOM from Onshape:

1. Open your Onshape document
2. Open the Assembly for your robot
3. Pick the **Bill of Materials** option (:Docs-Onshape-B-O-M-Tool) from the toolbar at the far right of the graphics area 
4. In the BOM panel that appears, select the Structured mode in order to properly capture quantities
5. Open the menu in the top right of the BOM panel and select **Export CSV**
6. Save the file to your computer

::tip
Export from your top-level assembly to capture all parts in your design, including quantities from sub-assemblies.
::

## CSV Format

The Onshape BOM export includes columns that Innovators Parts automatically recognizes:

| Onshape Column  | Used For                                 |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Part Number** | SKU matching with connected vendors      |
| **Quantity**    | Number of units to order                 |
| **Description** | Additional context for the part          |

::note
Exporting from Onshape is not required- any CSV with these columns can be used to import orders.
::

## Importing Your BOM

1. Click the **Import** button in the dashboard
2. Upload your Onshape CSV export
3. Innovators Parts will automatically search for matching products from your connected vendors
4. Review and adjust the matches:
   - Products with matching SKUs are auto-selected
   - Manually search and select products for unmatched rows
   - Choose the correct variant if multiple exist
   - Adjust quantities as needed
   - Remove rows you don't want to import
5. Click **Import** to create every part at once

The imported parts are grouped into orders by vendor, exactly as if you had added
them one at a time — so a BOM spanning four suppliers becomes four orders ready
to check out.



::note
Innovators Parts automatically combines duplicate rows in your CSV—if the same part number and description appear multiple times, their quantities are summed together.
::
